Frequently asked
Who can attend?
Young single adults ages 18–36. Invite friends! Use promo code TAGALONG2026 when bringing someone new.
How much does it cost and how do I register?
Registration is $50. Sign up at churchevents.eventsair.com/2026utahysaconference. Concert seating is limited, so register early.
Where is it held?
Salt Palace Convention Center in downtown Salt Lake City — 100 S West Temple, Salt Lake City, UT 84101. Multiple paid parking lots are nearby; check the venue's site for the latest options.
What's on the schedule?
Christ-centered devotionals with inspired speakers, interactive workshops on discipleship and faith-based relationships, service projects, social events, live musical performances, and dances both nights. Exact session times will be posted on the conference site closer to the event.
What should I wear and bring?
Sunday or smart-casual clothes that work for both worship and dancing. Comfortable shoes, a water bottle, journal, and scriptures. Most of all — an open heart and a willingness to meet new friends.
Who organizes this?
The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, Utah Area YSA. Reach out to poe@churchofjesuschrist.org with specific questions.
