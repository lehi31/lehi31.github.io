  2. What's New

Recent improvements to lehi31.com.

  1. July 24, 2026

    Sync the ward calendar to your own

    A new Subscribe button on the Calendar page connects Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook. Ward events flow into your personal calendar and stay up to date automatically — including changes and cancellations.

  2. July 24, 2026

    Church calendar changes are flagged

    When an event imported from the church calendar changes at the source — a new time, location, or title — the import review now shows a "Needs Update" tab with a side-by-side comparison, so nothing drifts out of date unnoticed.

  3. July 24, 2026

    Calling releases, start to finish

    Releasing someone from a calling is now a tracked workflow: begin the release, record when it was announced in sacrament meeting, and report it to LCR. Existing callings that predate the app can be recorded directly as active so they can be released too.

  4. July 24, 2026

    A simpler home page

    The signed-in home page now leads with Quick Links and your action items, with more destinations one tap away.

  5. July 23, 2026

    Clerks get full access

    The executive secretary and ward clerk roles now have the same access as the bishopric, and every role change is recorded in the activity log.

  6. July 22, 2026

    Missionaries, front and center

    Missionaries have their own section in the navigation: ward missionaries currently serving, returned missionaries, and contact emails for those assigned to our ward. Serving missionaries move to the returned list automatically when they come home.

  7. July 20, 2026

    Ward activity log

    Leadership can now see who changed what, and when — edits to events, callings, members, and settings are recorded and viewable on the Activity page.

  8. July 19, 2026

    Calendar list view

    The calendar now has a list view toggle beside the month grid — easier to scan upcoming events on a phone. Your choice is remembered.

  9. July 19, 2026

    Callings pipeline improvements

    The callings board now groups by stage, shows which steps are waiting on you specifically, and replaces the shared notes field with an authored discussion thread so you can see who said what.

  10. July 13, 2026

    Cancelled events stay visible

    Events can be marked cancelled instead of deleted. They stay on the calendar with a clear Cancelled badge, so everyone who saw the original announcement also sees the change.

  11. July 7, 2026

    Stronger account protections

    Registration now includes invisible anti-bot checks after a wave of spam sign-ups. Real people notice nothing; bots get nowhere.

  12. July 5, 2026

    Callings for couples

    Callings that go to a couple can now be linked and move through the approval workflow together. The member picker also shows age and gender to help tell members with similar names apart.

  13. June 14, 2026

    Import from the church calendar

    Ward leadership can pull events straight from the official church calendar feed and review them one by one — choose an organization, import or skip each event, and remove ones that no longer apply. Approved events publish automatically.

  14. June 10, 2026

    Events get their own pages

    Every event can now have its own shareable page with a photo gallery, multiple attachments, and a proper location — a map link for in-person events or a join link for virtual ones.

  15. May 17, 2026

    Members-only event details

    Events can carry notes and attachments visible only to signed-in members, so leadership can share details that do not belong on the public calendar.

  16. April 14, 2026

    A fresh new look

    A redesigned landing page featuring a painting of Christ, with the new colors and styling carried through the whole site in both dark and light mode.

  17. March 30, 2026

    Callings become a pipeline

    The callings tool was rebuilt as a step-by-step pipeline — propose, approve, extend, sustain, set apart — with permissions so each step is handled by the right person.

  18. March 1, 2026

    High council speakers and bishopric assignments

    Two new pages: the high council speaking schedule for the year, and bishopric assignments for conducting and youth schedules.

  19. February 25, 2026

    A more useful directory

    Member details gained call and text buttons, nicknames that show up in search, and smarter duplicate handling on import. The directory also works much better on phones.

  20. February 20, 2026

    Tell us what you think

    A floating feedback button lets anyone send a suggestion or report a problem from any page.

  21. February 16, 2026

    A true month calendar

    Events moved into a real month-grid calendar with day selection, plus a single search across events and announcements with matching text highlighted.

  22. February 14, 2026

    Homepage redesign

    The homepage now leads with upcoming events and a quick-links grid, and the site defaults to dark mode.

  23. February 12, 2026

    Private discussion on submissions

    Bishopric members can discuss an event in a live private thread right on the event, and submitters can go back and forth with the bishopric about their submission. Leadership can also bulk-import events from a spreadsheet.

  24. February 5, 2026

    Bishopric approval for events

    New events and announcements now go through bishopric approval before publishing, and a My Submissions view shows where each one stands.

  25. January 5, 2026

    Member directory

    Leadership can manage a member directory — import from a spreadsheet, organize member lists, and send a group text to a list.

  26. January 4, 2026

    Book of Mormon reading challenge

    Track your daily Book of Mormon reading right on the site, with the current reading front and center.

  27. December 16, 2025

    Sign up with email

    Create an account with your email address — verification emails and password reset included.

  28. November 17, 2025

    New navigation, roles, and permissions

    A redesigned navigation with a proper mobile drawer, plus an in-app roles and permissions system so access follows your calling.

  29. October 25, 2025

    Repeating events

    Events can repeat weekly, monthly, or yearly, with individual occurrences editable or removable on their own.

  30. October 20, 2025

    Flyers and photos on events

    Attach images to events — flyers show as thumbnails and are compressed automatically so pages stay fast.

  31. October 18, 2025

    Lesson schedule and social media

    New resource pages: the Sunday lesson schedule grouped by month, and links to ward social media accounts.

  32. April 10, 2025

    Temple Challenge

    Log your temple attendance for the ward Temple Challenge and watch the ward's combined total grow.

  33. March 19, 2025

    Assignments page

    A public assignments page covering bread, sacrament preparation, and other recurring duties — visible to everyone, editable by leadership.

  34. October 24, 2024

    A real calendar takes shape

    Announcements grew into full calendar events with organizations, details, and dates — the foundation of today's ward calendar.

  35. September 24, 2024

    Sacrament program goes live

    The weekly sacrament program is now edited on the site itself and published instantly — hymns, speakers, and ward business included.

  36. August 9, 2024

    Announcements and a home page

    The site gained a home page with ward announcements and a Book of Mormon challenge widget.

  37. August 5, 2024

    lehi31.com launches

    The very first version of the site: a simple home for the weekly sacrament program.
