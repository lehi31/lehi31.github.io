The Huddle

Counsel from the Lehi Utah 3rd Stake correlation training on the weekly missionary coordination meeting.

Held Sunday, August 9, 2026, 7:00 to 8:00 AM
Lehi Utah 3rd Stake — bishoprics, ward leaders, and ward missionaries
American Fork Stake Center, 680 North 350 West, American Fork, Utah

Notes from the stake correlation training. Counsel came from the stake presidency and stake missionary leadership, with bishops and ward leaders answering questions and describing what works in their own wards. The training also ran a live practice huddle using real ward members; nothing from that part is repeated here.

A huddle is not a meeting. Ten minutes, often standing: what happened last week, who needs something this week, and who is going to do it. Held every week it accomplishes far more than an hour-long meeting held once a month.

Who should be there

The invitation listed these callings by name, and the handbook says the same.

  • A member of the bishopric
  • The ward mission leader
  • The elders quorum presidency member over missionary work
  • The Relief Society presidency member over missionary work
  • An assistant in the priests quorum
  • A presidency member of the Young Women Gatherers of Light
  • A Primary presidency member
  • Ward missionaries
  • The full-time missionaries

Where a ward has no priests, the teachers or deacons quorum president comes instead. General Handbook 23.4 is the section to read.

What a huddle is

The shape of the meeting itself — how often it happens, how long it runs, and who is in it.

Hold it weekly Weekly is the instruction, not a suggestion.
Keep it to about ten minutes Football players huddle for fifteen seconds, not an hour.
Start on time, every time Do not hold the meeting hostage to whoever is late.
Get the right people in it Every ward should have a ward mission leader.

What happens in it

How to counsel about a person so that something actually changes before next Sunday.

Talk about the person, not the name A roster is not the point of the meeting.
Leave with a specific plan Discussion without an assignment simply repeats itself.
Act beyond the meeting The huddle is ten minutes. The work is the other six days.
Involve the youth They already know who is struggling.

Tools, and staying with it

What to track it in, how to find the time, and why it is worth doing badly rather than not at all.

Use the Covenant Path tools Most leaders already have access and have never opened it.
You have ten minutes The objection is almost never really about time.
Consistency over perfection Some weeks two of you show up. Hold it anyway.
It is not a program The huddle exists for the people in it and the people named in it.

United in purpose. Stronger together.
