Notes from the Young Men session. Brother Mendoza García opened and closed; President Sloan presented the area vision in between, and stake presidents and bishops shared their own experiences. Quotes are given as they were shared in the meeting.

Asked again and again what the new program is, the answer given was that there is no new program. The work is helping each person become a lifelong disciple of Jesus Christ — one by one, by name.
