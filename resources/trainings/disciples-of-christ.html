Disciples of Christ

Counsel from the General Advisory Training, grouped by theme so it can be read a piece at a time.

Held Thursday, August 6, 2026 at 7:00 PM
Lehi East Coordinating Council — stake and ward leaders
Young Men session with Brother Daniel Mendoza García, Young Men General Advisory Council
Lehi Utah Cedar Hollow Stake Center, 2178 North 1200 East, Lehi, Utah 84043

Notes from the Young Men session. Brother Mendoza García opened and closed; President Sloan presented the area vision in between, and stake presidents and bishops shared their own experiences. Quotes are given as they were shared in the meeting.

Asked again and again what the new program is, the answer given was that there is no new program. The work is helping each person become a lifelong disciple of Jesus Christ — one by one, by name.

Becoming a disciple

Where discipleship starts: how we see each other, why the Resurrection matters now, and whose counsel we follow.

See everyone as a child of God Love that does not depend on agreement, sameness, or being treated well first.
Live worthy to meet Christ The Resurrection is a pillar of our faith, and believing it changes the whole perspective of mortal life.
Follow living prophets and apostles There are great blessings in aligning both our hearts and our actions with living prophets, seers, and revelators.

Gathering one by one

The Utah Area invitation, what it looks like when a ward follows it, and why we do not give up on anyone.

Trim back the bushes President Brian K. Taylor of the Utah Area Presidency: find the ones who are hidden in the shadows.
Keep ministering — you do not know when they will return Two people who came back long after anyone might have given up on them.
Missionaries are peacemakers When we send missionaries out, we are sending a message from the Prince of Peace.

The rising generation

What the training asked of those who teach, advise, and ordain our youth.

Raise peacemakers, not just rule-keepers What it actually looks like to trade self-gratifying activities for service and kind acts.
Help every young man become a lifelong disciple The answer to "what is the new young men program?" is that there is not one.
Ordination to elder: start before 18 Handbook guidance, and a practice one leader shared for getting ahead of it.
What changes on Sundays in September The For the Strength of Youth guide expands from five sections to twelve, and Sunday follows a new monthly pattern.
