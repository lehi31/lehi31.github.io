What each organization studies each Sunday this month and next. Tap a Sunday to open that lesson. Sunday School (adults and youth) and Primary follow Come, Follow Me; Young Women and Aaronic Priesthood use For the Strength of Youth; Elders Quorum and Relief Society study an assigned general conference talk.

Youth study one For the Strength of Youth chapter each month. Each Sunday’s lesson outline and related stories and activities are in the magazine; the doctrine is in the guide.
