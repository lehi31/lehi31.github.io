Start here

Call now if you need help

  • Call 911 Immediate danger or a medical emergency.
  • Call or text 988 Mental health or suicide crisis support.
  • Dial 211 Utah 211 gives free, confidential referrals for food, housing, utilities, employment, transportation and healthcare.
  • Text your ZIP code to Utah 211 Text to 801-845-2211

Identify the most urgent need, then call the listed agency. Ask about eligibility, documents to bring, current hours and whether you need an appointment. If you need Church assistance, contact the bishop privately.

Self-reliance is not about doing everything alone. It is about having faith to move forward, courage to ask for help, and determination to become stronger so that one day we can lift others.

Rent and housing

Call Utah 211 and ask about current rental assistance, eviction prevention, shelters, housing counseling and Utah County referrals. Funding and waitlists change.

211

HEAT utility assistance

Utah’s Home Energy Assistance Target program helps eligible households with heating and cooling costs. Call 211 for a local intake agency.

211 HEAT on jobs.utah.gov

Community Action Services

Ask about current housing stabilization, budgeting education and local support programs.

801-373-8200

Financial triage

Prioritize food, safe housing, utilities, transportation to work and essential medications. Contact lenders and utility providers early to request hardship options, and avoid high-cost payday loans when possible.

Family budget starter

Write down your monthly take-home income. List your essential bills. Track your spending for 30 days. Pick one expense to reduce. Build a small emergency fund as circumstances allow.

Church welfare: how to ask for help

Contact your bishop

The bishop directs ward welfare assistance and can discuss immediate needs confidentially, including possible food or other basic necessities.

Relief Society and elders quorum

The presidencies can minister, identify needs, help with practical plans, and connect households to community and Church resources while respecting privacy.

Bishops’ storehouse

Food and basic goods may be available through a bishop’s order. Do not visit expecting walk-in assistance without a bishop’s arrangement.

Preparing for natural disasters

Know local hazards

Earthquakes, winter storms, severe weather, power outages, wildfires and flooding can affect Utah County. Check Be Ready Utah and your city’s emergency alerts.

Be Ready Utah

Family plan

Choose two meeting places (one near home and one outside your neighborhood), an out-of-area contact, evacuation routes, and a way to reconnect if phones fail.

Earthquake

Drop, Cover, and Hold On during shaking, and practice with children. Secure heavy furniture and water heaters appropriately.

Wildfire and evacuation

Know two routes out, keep your vehicle’s fuel adequate, and prepare a grab-and-go bag. Follow official evacuation instructions.

Winter and power outages

Keep warm layers, blankets, flashlights, batteries, charged power banks and safe heating practices. Never use a generator indoors.
